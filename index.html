Privacy Policy (Summary)

MomuBloom by MASARP STUDIO stores all personal data locally on your device. We do not transmit your name, age, medical details, or emergency contacts to any server. Anonymous, opt-in analytics help us improve the app. You may delete all data at any time from Settings.

🚨 Emergency Help

Seek immediate care if you experience:
  • Severe headache or vision changes
  • Heavy bleeding or gush of fluid
  • Chest pain or difficulty breathing
  • Decreased or absent fetal movement
  • High fever or severe abdominal pain
  • Thoughts of harming yourself or baby
Source: CDC HEAR HER Campaign
