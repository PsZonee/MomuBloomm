Privacy Policy (Summary)

MomuBloom by MASARP STUDIO & Research Dhamala stores all personal data locally on your device using secure browser storage. We do not transmit your name, age, medical details, or emergency contacts to any server. Anonymous, opt-in analytics help us improve the app but contain no personally identifiable information. You may delete all your data at any time from Settings. By using this app you confirm you are 18+ or using under adult supervision. Content is sourced from WHO, CDC, ACOG, and NHS. This app does not replace your prenatal care team.

Data Collected: Profile information (name, age, due date, allergies, medications), appointment records, and symptom check history — all stored locally on your device only.

Permissions: We may request notification permissions (for medication reminders) and phone call access (for SOS emergency feature only, with your explicit confirmation).

🚨 Emergency Help

Seek immediate care if you experience:
  • Severe headache or vision changes
  • Heavy bleeding or gush of fluid
  • Chest pain or difficulty breathing
  • Decreased or absent fetal movement
  • High fever or severe abdominal pain
  • Thoughts of harming yourself or baby
Source: CDC HEAR HER Campaign · Maternal Warning Signs
